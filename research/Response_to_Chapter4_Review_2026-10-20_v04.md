## What the chapter now makes explicit

- On corrected case-base data, equal-weight medical retrieval changes from −337.07% under type pooling to +10.26% under purpose pooling at p = .50, and reaches +22.40% at p75. The pool comparison holds the corrected outcomes fixed, so it directly supports the diagnosed cross-purpose mismatch within this synthetic evaluation.
- At p75, all five non-transport types beat their condition-specific purpose baselines. Four of those five have strictly increasing reductions across all four conditions; food dips from p00 to p25. This is evidence of signal-responsive retrieval, distinct from evidence of successful learned household-weight recovery.
- The fixed tests still fail: original −42.74%, corrected type pool −49.05%, corrected purpose pool −5.13%. The fourteen corrected medical fixed cases still trail their own baseline by 63.08%. The case-base success is not substituted for these results.
- The equal-weight k-table objectives fall monotonically over the tested 3, 5, 7 and 10 neighbors in both pools at p = .50. Section 4.17 now discusses the smaller-median variability interpretation and names alternative explanations rather than claiming a measured variance mechanism.

## Qualifications to the review

“Four of five non-medical types” should read “four of five non-transport types.” There are four non-medical, non-transport types, not five. Transportation ties its zero-error baseline and does not beat it.

The purpose baseline and household-selected neighbors use the same form of share/amount summary, but do not necessarily estimate the same conditional quantity when household effects exist. Larger-k improvement is consistent with a variance trade-off; it does not prove variance dominance or discredit the similarity measure. The fixed-case fluctuations are not demonstrated to be sampling noise, and the study does not establish that signal strength is the only remaining limitation or that retrieval has no flaws.

Use “fixed in advance” rather than imply a publicly registered protocol. The post-failure amendments remain explicitly dated and disclosed. The primary purpose-pool weight tests remain non-significant (p = .246–.534); replication 51024 remains infeasible and no 29-set noise-floor substitute is introduced.
